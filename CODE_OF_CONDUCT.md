# AI Society Code of Conduct

**University of Portsmouth**

The AI Society is a student-led community where people come together to learn, explore and build with artificial intelligence.

We want everyone to feel welcome, respected and comfortable participating, regardless of their background, experience or technical knowledge.

## Our principles

We ask everyone to:

- **Be respectful.** Treat others with kindness and consideration, including when you disagree.
- **Be inclusive.** Welcome different perspectives, backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone should feel able to participate.
- **Be collaborative.** Share knowledge, give constructive feedback and support others in learning.
- **Be responsible.** Respect people's privacy, personal boundaries and intellectual property.
- **Be open-minded.** Encourage curiosity, thoughtful discussion and respectful debate about AI and its wider impact.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, threats and personal attacks are not tolerated.

This also includes unwanted sexual attention, deliberately disrupting activities, sharing someone's private information without permission, or continuing behaviour after being asked to stop.

Disagreement and constructive criticism are welcome. They must remain respectful.

## Where this applies

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone participating in society activities, including members, committee members, speakers, guests and collaborators.

It covers society-organised events, workshops, meetings, projects, GitHub repositories and society-managed online communities.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness behaviour that makes you or others feel unsafe or unwelcome, we encourage you to speak up.

You can approach the society's **Welfare Secretary** or another committee member you feel comfortable speaking to. Their role is to provide support and help you find an appropriate reporting route, not to conduct formal investigations.

For serious or sensitive concerns, particularly those involving committee members, you can report directly through the [Students' Union complaints procedure](https://upsu.net/your-union/information/grievance).

Please do not report personal or sensitive incidents through public GitHub Issues or community chats.

Concerns should be treated respectfully and discreetly, with appropriate action taken under the applicable Students' Union or University procedures. Retaliation against anyone raising a concern in good faith is unacceptable.

## Our commitment

The committee aims to maintain a safe, welcoming and constructive environment for everyone.

This document complements the [University of Portsmouth Students' Union's policies and Code of Conduct](https://upsu.net/your-union/information/useful-documents), which remain applicable to society members and activities.

We will review this document as needed to keep it relevant for future members and committees.
