# AI Society

**University of Portsmouth**

We're a student-led community at the University of Portsmouth bringing together people who are curious about artificial intelligence.

Whether you're just getting started with AI, interested in its impact on the world, or already building your own projects, there's a place for you here.

## What we're about

We're rebuilding the society for 2026/27 with a focus on learning by doing, sharing ideas and connecting people.

Here's what we're working towards:

- **Workshops & build nights** — Get hands-on with AI tools, software development and real-world applications.
- **Collaborative projects** — Experiment, build and learn alongside other students.
- **Talks & discussions** — Explore AI research, emerging technologies, startups and the impact of AI on society.
- **Industry & community events** — Connect with people working in AI, discover opportunities and share experiences.

## Our GitHub

This is where we'll share code, workshop materials, experiments and projects developed by our community.

As we build things together, you'll find them here.

## Get involved

You don't need to be an expert or even know how to code. Whether you want to learn, contribute to a project, share an idea or simply meet others interested in AI, you're welcome.
